Asset credits

CivicOps Field Training is illustrative practice, not an employer SOP and not a qualification. It does not record audio or video. No identifiable people or recorded speech are used. In a desktop browser, hand practice uses the IWER Quest 3 emulator with emulated tracked hands. A physical Quest uses native WebXR. This page is not a claim of real, unedited field capture.

All other equipment, street geometry, UI, sound synthesis and code are original to this training build, dedicated CC0 1.0 except for the third-party items below.

  • Barrier & Traffic Cone Pack by Sabri Ayeş (Sketchfab), CC BY 4.0. Modified: Selected cone, metre scale, resized PBR maps, meshopt and KTX2.
  • Utility Box 02 by James Ray Cock and Empty Workshop by Sergej Majboroda (Poly Haven), CC0. Cabinet reduced and compressed; photograph tone-mapped and ground-projected for emulator footage only, labelled as simulated passthrough.
  • Minibus base by Alvin.Woodly, CC BY 4.0. Modified: badges and manufacturer marks removed, generic grille, roof rack, generic destination board, reduced geometry. Also reused for the municipal cone van (destination board removed, service paint, amber beacon). Full provenance is in the repository file ASSETS.md.
  • Ultimate Modular Women by Quaternius, CC0 1.0. Generic worker rig. Modified: clothing, connected surfaces, reduced geometry and anonymous crew variations. Road marshals reuse this rig with navy workwear and original hand-attached STOP/GO paddles.
  • Asphalt 02 and Plastered Wall 02, Poly Haven, CC0. Resized PBR scans, packed trim atlas and KTX2 compression.
  • Kloofendal partly cloudy pure sky and Kloofendal misty morning, Poly Haven, CC0. Resized HDR skies and environmental lighting.
  • Small Workshop HDRI by Dimitrios Savva and Jarod Guest (Poly Haven), CC0. Resampled to 512×256 for indoor image-based lighting.
