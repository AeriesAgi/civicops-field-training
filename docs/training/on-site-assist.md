# Room rehearsal and on-site assist

Illustrative decision support; follow your employer's SOPs. A human decides every
work, traffic, medical and safety action. This module awards no professional authority.

**Learning outcomes:** choose a safe real-room placement; distinguish a detected
floor, table and wall; pin the work/step/NEXT board beside the job; recognise when
planes or persistent anchors are unavailable; use STOP, human recheck and deliberate
holds instead of skipping a safety gate; inspect/export the structured local log.

**Timebox:** five minutes for placement and one crew guide, then repeat without hints.
Native `immersive-ar` is requested at `?mode=assist`. The AR scene contains no virtual
street or sky dome. Its background belongs to the passthrough compositor. Optional
WebXR plane detection and anchors work through the same path on Quest and IWER.
The emulator uses the IWSDK synthetic meeting room; it is not footage of a real home.

**Programme:**

1. Briefing: clear the real room, choose seated/standing comfort, inspect PPE and
   retain the employer's authority. Enter from the board's AR option.
2. Select **Pin beside job**. Open the hand, point at a real floor/table/wall and
   pinch again. Near touch also works. The point must be inside a detected plane;
   the board is lifted from a floor or table, or faced out from a real wall.
3. Floor placement previews three full-size cones, a valve key and a 2.4×1.2 m
   trench exclusion outline. An isolation panel is placed beside the work board.
   These are rehearsal props, not a substitute for physical site protection.
4. With no suitable planes, pinch to place the board manually. **Floor − / +**
   adjusts the preview in 5 cm increments. Inspect real clearance before moving.
5. When available, the browser creates a native anchor and requests an opaque
   persistent handle. Reload restores that handle. Unsupported/revoked handles
   and blocked storage keep the guide usable and require re-placement.
6. Choose the crew job guide, hazard callouts or the situational-awareness option.
   At every critical step, confirm the actual human check, then hold for one
   second. **No / Not sure** enters stop-work; perform a human recheck before resuming.
7. The pinned board stays at the job. A small **Follow view** control appears when
   the board leaves the comfortable viewing cone. Follow view unpins/forgets the
   previous anchor so the crew can deliberately place it again.
8. Review the hash-chained structured step log and export locally if needed. No
   photos, video, audio, room scans, plane polygons, coordinates or person identity
   enter that log. The app stores only the native anchor handle for placement.

**Critical safety gates:** skipping the confirm/hold sequence is refused; uncertainty
stops the guide; recheck and a fresh hold are required to proceed. Mocked dispatch
and SOS are labelled simulated. Real lanes must be calibrated by the human; the
old synthetic street lane is not mapped onto the real room.

**Seated path:** hand rays allow floor placement without bending down. Pinch/poke,
one-handed mode, caption sizes, contrast, reduced motion and optional dwell remain
available. Re-place the board within comfortable reach; use Follow view when it is
out of sight. Actual headset comfort and unknown-room hardware behaviour remain
unverified without a Quest.

**Competency/debrief:** AR logs human-confirmed checks and stop/recheck outcomes.
The four field programmes own scored practice/certification. Room rehearsal does
not currently implement separate tool-task scoring or the requested deployment
rescue programmes. Cone/valve/panel props currently move as an anchored arrangement;
individual prop placement and robust automatic clearance fitting remain pending.
Native persistent anchors may require supported browser/device permissions and a
room setup. A manual placement is never labelled a detected surface.

**Supervisor refresher:** link `?mode=assist&workOrderType=ELECTRICITY|WATER|ROADS|FIRE`.
No dispatch message or external endpoint is called.
