# Fire / EMS: municipal crash-scene safety

Illustrative training based on generic municipal practice; follow your employer's
SOPs. A qualified human decides medical care, rescue and every scene-safety action.

**Learning outcomes:** stage clear of hazards; protect the approach; identify fuel,
electrical, instability and traffic hazards; maintain exclusion; practise triage
priorities with synthetic mannequins; communicate a structured handover.
**Competencies:** blocker/warning placement, scene size-up, live-line exclusion,
protected access, simulated triage and interdepartmental handover.
**Timebox:** 8 minutes; the scenario also contains a triage target. Accommodations
and employer protocols govern real work; no overall score rewards rushing safety.

## Shift brief and preparation

A crash sits on an arterial beside palisades, a trader stall, corrugated roofs,
minibuses and municipal services. There are no identifiable casualties or real
people. The downed line remains live until Electricity confirms isolation.

1. Confirm the human authority chain; this app cannot authorise rescue or treatment.
2. Reject touching/pulling a downed line or contacted vehicle.
3. Stage upwind/clear of fuel; never put ignition flares in the spill.
4. Preserve protected medical staging and an escape route.
5. Inspect suitable task PPE and medical gloves before contact.
6. Confirm that practice tags are not a diagnosis or authority to treat.

## Procedure gates and feedback

Angle the fire-engine blocker upstream of the crash. Place the taper and warning
flares in the simulated safe warning area. Identify all four hazards: fuel, line,
unstable car and open lane. Establish cable exclusion, then choose the protected
shoulder approach. Read each synthetic casualty card and hand-place the appropriate
shape-and-colour tag. Finish the handover card with persistent cable status and
required units. Seven independently checked procedure outcomes feed shared scoring.

The `seed=1` refresher changes one casualty's state and requires reassessment and
retagging; it does not diagnose any real person. Context hints explain the next
incomplete hazard, exclusion or handover step, with captions available offline.

## Critical fails and remediation

Pulling the line or introducing ignition into the spill instantly stops preparation.
Unsafe blocker placement, incomplete warning, disregarding a critical hazard,
unsafe approach, bypassed exclusion or unsafe triage outcomes stop the procedure.
Read the specific cause, make safe and repeat after human review. Correcting an
unsafe act never removes its failed evidence. No scene tag changes clinical authority.

Debrief: state the remaining line/fuel/traffic risks, protected access, casualty
priority changes and who needs to receive the handover. Review safety, accuracy
and pace using the [shared assessment ladder](README.md). Fire's Assistant/Operator/Supervisor
levels are labelled Levels 1–3 to avoid confusion with triage categories.

## Seated-compatible path and current limits

Use hand rays and one-hand grab placement. Teleport between protected scene
positions; do not require physical bending, walking or casualty lifting. Cards
and result review remain in the seated view. Full runtime, device FoV and natural
seated reach are still under verification. The app does not teach clinical
assessment, treatment, extrication technique or emergency-service qualification.
